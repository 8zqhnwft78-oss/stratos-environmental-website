Privacy & Cookie Policy

How we handle your information and use cookies on this website.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Who we are

Stratos Environmental ("we", "us") is a commercial waste comparison broker based in London, United Kingdom. You can contact us at info@stratosenvironmental.co.uk or on +44 7448 730416.

What data we collect

  • Quote & enquiry details you submit — company name, postcode, contact name, phone, email and your waste requirements.
  • Invoice uploads you choose to send us for comparison.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics about how the site is used (only if you accept cookies).

How we use your data

We use the details you provide solely to prepare your waste collection quote, compare providers on your behalf, and contact you about your enquiry. We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to measure website traffic and improve advertising relevance. Non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising, including Google Ads) are only set after you accept via our cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies for this site, which will show the banner again.

We use Google Consent Mode, which means Google's tags respect your choice and default to a non-personalised, cookieless mode until you accept.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email info@stratosenvironmental.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to provide our service and meet legal obligations, after which it is securely deleted.
