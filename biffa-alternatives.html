Biffa is one of the UK’s largest and best-known waste management companies, serving businesses nationwide. It’s an established, reputable operator — but being one of the biggest doesn’t automatically mean it’s the cheapest or the best fit for every business. If you’re wondering whether you could get a better deal, Stratos Environmental Ltd compares the whole market, including Biffa and a wide range of other licensed carriers, so you can make an informed choice.

Why businesses look for a Biffa alternative

  • Price — to check their rates are still competitive after years of renewals and annual increases.
  • Contract terms — some businesses prefer more flexibility on length or notice periods.
  • Service fit — a smaller or more local carrier sometimes suits a particular site better.
  • Simple due diligence — it’s good practice to benchmark any long-standing supplier periodically.

How we compare

We’re an independent broker, not a Biffa competitor trying to knock them — we simply put licensed providers side by side on price, service and terms. The easiest way to see where you stand is to upload your current Biffa invoice. We’ll benchmark it against our partner network and tell you honestly whether we can beat it. If Biffa is already giving you a great deal, we’ll say so.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch from Biffa to another provider?

Yes. As with any waste contract, you can switch once you're within your notice period. Check your contract's end date and notice terms first — we can help you plan the timing and handle the setup with a new provider.

Will you be able to beat my Biffa price?

Often, but not always — it depends on your current deal. Upload your Biffa invoice and we'll benchmark it honestly against our licensed partner network. If your existing price is already competitive, we'll tell you.

Is Stratos affiliated with Biffa?

No. We're an independent comparison broker. We compare many licensed carriers, which may or may not include Biffa depending on your location and needs.

How do I compare Biffa prices?

The simplest way is to upload your current Biffa invoice or request a free quote. We'll compare it like-for-like against other licensed providers.
