360 Litre Wheelie Bin

A mid-small commercial wheelie bin — about 5–6 bags. The step up from 240L for busier shops and small cafes.

Get a Free Quote

The 360 litre wheelie bin sits between a 240L and a 660L: more capacity than a small office bin without the footprint of a large commercial container. It is a common choice for cafes, small retailers and offices that outgrew 240L. We compare 360L collections across licensed partners.

Capacity and typical use

  • About 5–6 bin bags per lift
  • Busy small shops, cafes, clinics and larger offices
  • Useful when 240L overflows but a 660L will not fit

Check doorway and store width before you commit — 360L bins are taller/bulkier than 240L. If access is tight, more frequent 240L collections can be easier.

Frequently asked questions

When should I choose 360L instead of 240L?

When a 240L is full before collection day or you are paying for extra bags. If the 360L still overflows, look at 660L or a second weekly collection.

Will a 360L fit through a standard door?

Measure first. 360L bins are bulkier than 240L and can be awkward in narrow corridors or basement stores.

Can 360L be used for recycling?

Yes. Many sites run a 360L recycling bin next to a general waste bin of the same or different size.
