240 Litre Wheelie Bin

The smallest common commercial wheelie bin — about 3–4 bin bags. Ideal for small offices, salons and shops. Compare collection prices free.

A 240 litre wheelie bin is the standard small commercial container: roughly three to four black bags, similar in footprint to a large household bin but on a business contract. It is the right starting point for low-volume premises. Stratos Environmental compares licensed collections for 240L general waste and recycling so small sites are not pushed into an oversized 1100L.

Capacity and typical use

  • About 3–4 bin bags per lift
  • Best for small offices, salons, studios and quiet shops
  • Common for recycling alongside a larger general bin

If you overflow a 240L every week, step up to a 360L or collect twice weekly rather than leaving side waste (which gets charged).

Indicative cost

As a rough guide, 240L general waste is often around £6–£12 per collection excluding VAT and standing charges. See our prices guide and size guide.

Frequently asked questions

How many bags fit in a 240L bin?

Typically around three to four standard bin bags, depending how tightly they are packed and whether they are compacted.

Is a 240L bin cheaper than a 660L?

Per lift, yes. If you fill a 240L and need many extra collections, a larger bin collected less often can work out cheaper. We'll compare both.

Can I use a 240L for food waste?

Some sites do; many kitchens use smaller food caddies. It depends on volume and what the carrier supplies.
