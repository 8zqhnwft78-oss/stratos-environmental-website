London restaurants generate some of the most demanding waste of any business: large volumes of food waste, heavy glass, packaging and general rubbish — often produced late and needing frequent, well-timed collection. Get it wrong and you face hygiene problems, pests, fines and complaints; get it right and it’s clean, compliant and cost-controlled. Stratos Environmental Ltd compares licensed providers to build a collection that fits how your restaurant actually runs.

The waste streams a restaurant needs

  • Food waste — separate collection is a legal requirement and keeps your general waste (the priciest stream) lighter. Essential for kitchens.
  • Glass — bottles from the bar collected separately and safely.
  • Dry mixed recycling — cardboard, packaging, cans and plastics.
  • General waste — what’s left after recycling and food are separated.

Frequency and timing

Restaurants often need multiple collections a week — and in central London, daily. Because kitchens produce food waste that quickly becomes a hygiene and pest issue, and because many boroughs restrict when waste can be presented, timing matters as much as volume. We arrange frequencies and collection windows that work around your service and your borough’s rules, including the timed-collection regimes common in Westminster and the West End.

Compliance for restaurants

You must separate recyclables and food waste under Simpler Recycling, use a registered carrier, and keep waste transfer notes. We handle all of that. See our guide to restaurant waste regulations for the full picture.

Cutting costs without cutting corners

Food waste is heavy, so separating it properly is one of the best ways to control the general-waste bill. Right-sizing bins and matching frequency to trade does the rest. Frequently asked questions

How often should a London restaurant have waste collected?

Most restaurants need several collections a week, and busy central London sites often need daily collection of food waste and glass. The right frequency depends on covers, menu and storage — we'll match it to your service.

Do London restaurants legally have to separate food waste?

Yes. Under Simpler Recycling, workplaces must separate food waste and dry recyclables from general waste. Restaurants with kitchens should treat food waste as a core, frequent stream.

Can you work around timed collections in central London?

Yes. We match you with carriers that collect within the time bands imposed by boroughs like Westminster, so you stay compliant on busy streets.
