Cookie Policy

What cookies we use and how to control them.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites work, remember your preferences, and measure how the site is used.

How we use cookies

We only set non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) after you accept them via our cookie banner. Until then, our tags run in a privacy-preserving, cookieless mode using Google Consent Mode.

Categories of cookies we use

CategoryPurposeSet byRequires consent?
EssentialCore site functionality and remembering your cookie choice.Stratos Environmental LtdNo
AdvertisingMeasuring ad performance (conversions) and remarketing via Google Ads.Google (gtag.js)Yes
AnalyticsUnderstanding how visitors use the site so we can improve it.GoogleYes

Managing your consent

When you first visit, our banner lets you Accept or Reject non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's cookies and site data in your browser — the banner will then reappear so you can choose again.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works.

Google Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode v2. This means Google's advertising and analytics tags respect your choice: when you reject, no advertising or analytics cookies are stored and Google relies on aggregated, cookieless modelling instead.

More information

For how we handle your personal data, see our Privacy & Cookie Policy. You can learn more about cookies at the ICO's website, ico.org.uk.
