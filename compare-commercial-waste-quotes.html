Compare Commercial Waste Quotes

Compare quotes from licensed waste providers across the UK in minutes — free, no obligation, and we’ll try to beat your current price.

Get a Free Quote Upload Your Invoice

Ringing round waste companies one by one is slow, and it’s almost impossible to tell whether the price you’re quoted is competitive. Stratos Environmental Ltd does the legwork for you: tell us what you need once, and we compare licensed providers across our network to find the best combination of price, service and reliability for your business.

Why compare?

  • Prices vary widely — the same collection can cost very different amounts depending on the carrier.
  • Contracts creep up — if you’ve been with the same supplier for years, you’re probably overpaying after successive annual increases.
  • One conversation, whole market — we do the comparison so you don’t have to chase multiple suppliers.
  • Independent advice — we’re not tied to one provider, so we recommend what genuinely suits you.

What we compare

We look at the whole picture, not just the headline rate: per-collection price, bin rental and standing charges, contract length and notice periods, collection frequency, and the specific waste streams you need — general waste, recycling, food waste, glass and specialist streams.

What you’ll need

To compare quotes we just need a few details: your business type and postcode, the waste types you produce, a rough idea of volumes or current bin sizes, and how often you need collections. If you have a current invoice, even better — upload it and we’ll benchmark it directly.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need — a quick form or your current invoice.
  2. We compare providers — across our licensed partner network.
  3. You choose — we present the best options and, once you’re happy, set everything up.

No obligation, ever

Comparing costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. If we can’t beat your current deal, we’ll tell you straight. See indicative costs on our prices guide, or start now.

Frequently asked questions

Is comparing waste quotes really free?

Yes. The comparison is completely free and there's no obligation to switch. If we can't improve on your current arrangement, we'll tell you honestly.

How long does it take to get quotes?

Usually just a couple of minutes to submit your details, and a specialist will come back to you quickly — often the same day — with your options.

Do I have to switch if I get a quote?

No. There's no obligation. You only proceed if you're happy with the price and service on offer.

What if I'm still in contract with my current provider?

We can still benchmark your pricing and plan the switch for when your notice period allows. Upload your invoice and we'll advise on timing.
