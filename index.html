FAQs

Commercial waste questions, answered

Everything you need to know about comparing commercial waste providers with Stratos Environmental Ltd.

Are you a waste collection company?

No — we do not operate a collection fleet on this site. Stratos Environmental Ltd is an Environment Agency–registered upper tier waste broker and dealer (CBDU661179). We compare quotes from licensed collection partners; collections are carried out by registered waste carriers.

How do you get paid?

Like most brokers, we are remunerated by the provider when you take a service. That does not change the fact we compare multiple options and will tell you if we cannot beat your current price.

How much does commercial waste collection cost?

Prices depend on waste type, bin size and collection frequency. Share your details or upload your current invoice and we'll compare providers to find you the best price — free and no obligation.

What types of business waste can you compare?

General waste, dry mixed recycling, food waste, paper & cardboard, glass, and clinical/healthcare waste. We can compare multiple waste streams under one arranged contract with a licensed partner.

How quickly can collections be set up?

Often the same day. Once you've chosen your preferred quote, our partner arranges bin delivery and your first collection with minimal disruption.

Can you beat my current waste bill?

Very often, yes. Upload your current waste invoice and we'll compare it against our network of trusted suppliers to see if we can save you money.

Are collections compliant with waste regulations?

Yes. All collections are arranged through registered waste carriers and are fully Duty of Care compliant, with the documentation you need.

I'm already a customer — how do I order more sacks?

Use the customer portal to order more sacks or extra collections. It emails the team straight away so we can order from the supplier. You can still call +44 7448 730416 if you need it urgently.
