Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to use our website.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Our commitment

Stratos Environmental Ltd is committed to making its website accessible to as many people as possible, regardless of ability or technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What we do

  • Use semantic HTML and clear heading structure so screen readers can navigate the site.
  • Provide descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images and icons.
  • Maintain sufficient colour contrast for text and interactive elements.
  • Ensure the site is responsive and usable on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Support keyboard navigation for links, buttons and forms.

Known limitations

We are continually improving the site. Some third-party content or older documents may not yet fully meet our accessibility goals. If you encounter a barrier, please let us know and we will do our best to help and to fix it.
