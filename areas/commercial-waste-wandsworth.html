Typical waste setups for Wandsworth businesses

An independent café on Old York Road or Bellevue Road usually needs a 240L or 360L general bin alongside dry mixed recycling and a food caddy, collected two to three times a week. A restaurant near Wandsworth Town or in the Ram Quarter typically runs 660L or 1100L general waste with separate food and glass, collected several times weekly. A trade or light-industrial unit off Garratt Lane often wants larger general or FEL capacity plus cardboard recycling, while retailers in Southside generate high cardboard volumes best handled with frequent dry mixed recycling. We size each setup to the specific site rather than defaulting to the biggest bin.

Getting the frequency right in SW18

Because storage is tight across much of Wandsworth — think converted Victorian units and shared rear yards — the cheapest option is rarely the largest bin; it’s the right combination of container and frequency. We look at how quickly you actually fill up and where you can realistically keep bins, then recommend a schedule that avoids both overflowing bins (and the side-waste charges that follow) and paying for half-empty collections. If your current contract has crept up in price, we’ll check your notice period and manage the switch for you.

Staying compliant in Wandsworth

In England, workplace recycling rules took effect on 31 March 2025. Micro-firms with fewer than 10 full-time equivalent employees across the whole business have until 31 March 2027. Businesses must also check their waste carrier is registered and retain non-hazardous waste transfer notes for at least two years. See the official Simpler Recycling guidance for separation requirements and the micro-firm exemption. We handle that paperwork as part of setting up your collection, so compliance is one less thing to worry about.
