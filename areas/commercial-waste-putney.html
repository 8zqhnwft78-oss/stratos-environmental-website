Typical waste setups for Putney businesses

A High Street retailer usually needs frequent cardboard and dry mixed recycling plus a modest general bin. A riverside pub or restaurant near the Embankment needs heavy glass and food waste with several collections a week, rising sharply around the Boat Race and through the summer. A professional-services office on Upper or Lower Richmond Road typically wants tidy mixed recycling and confidential waste with a smaller general stream. We build the collection around whichever of these you are.

Getting the frequency right in SW15

Putney’s high street has limited kerbside space and busy pavements, so the right answer is usually a well-judged frequency rather than an oversized bin. For hospitality with seasonal peaks, we can flex collections up and down rather than locking you into a rigid year-round schedule — so you’re not paying peak rates in the quiet months. Where storage is very tight, timed sack collections can work better than wheelie bins.
