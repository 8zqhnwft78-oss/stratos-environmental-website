Salon Waste Collection

Waste collection for hair and beauty salons — compact bins, recycling, sanitary units and clear advice on chemicals and blades.

Get a Free Quote Upload Your Invoice

Salons are small premises with a surprising mix of waste: hair and general rubbish, product packaging, washroom sanitary waste, and sometimes blades or chemical residues that cannot go in the general bin. Stratos Environmental Ltd compares licensed collections sized for a high-street salon — usually 240L or 360L — rather than restaurant-scale 1100L bins you have nowhere to store.

Typical salon streams

  • General waste (including hair, which is not recyclable in standard DMR)
  • Dry mixed recycling for clean bottles, cans and card
  • Sanitary units for the staff/client toilet
  • Blades/razors as sharps if you use them

Hair dye, solvents and aerosol cans that are not empty can be hazardous. We will not tell you to pour chemicals in the general bin; where our partners cannot take a hazardous stream we will say so.

High-street storage

Most salons need a small bin collected weekly or twice weekly, plus recycling if space allows. Timed sack collections can work where there is no yard at all.

Frequently asked questions

Can hair go in recycling?

Not in standard dry mixed recycling. Hair belongs in general waste unless you have a specialist organics arrangement.

Do salons need sharps collection?

Only if you use blades, razors or similar. Many hair salons do not; beauty clinics that use needles or blades do.

What bin size suits a salon?

Usually a 240L or 360L general waste bin, collected weekly. We'll confirm based on chairs and how much packaging you generate.
