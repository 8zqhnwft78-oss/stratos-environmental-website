Nursery & Childcare Waste Collection

Practical waste collection for nurseries and preschools — nappies, food waste and recycling — compared across licensed providers.

Nurseries generate a lot of nappies (offensive/hygiene waste), leftover food from meals and snacks, plus recycling from deliveries and activities. Collections need to be reliable: a missed nappy collection is a hygiene problem, not just an inconvenience. Stratos Environmental Ltd compares licensed partners who can combine offensive, food and general/recycling streams for childcare settings.

Typical nursery streams

  • Nappies and hygiene waste in a dedicated bin or tiger-stripe bags
  • Food waste from lunches and snacks (Simpler Recycling applies)
  • Dry mixed recycling for cardboard and clean packaging
  • General waste for residual rubbish
  • Sanitary units for staff toilets

Nappies from healthy children are normally offensive waste, not clinical. Clinical classification is for infectious waste, not everyday childcare.

Access and safeguarding

Collections should not disrupt the outdoor play area or block fire exits. We note access constraints (gated sites, limited collection windows during drop-off) when we compare carriers.

Frequently asked questions

Are nappies clinical waste?

Nappies from healthy children are normally offensive or hygiene waste, not clinical. Clinical applies if waste is infectious.

Do nurseries need food waste collection?

Yes under Simpler Recycling if you produce food waste from meals and snacks. A small food caddy collected weekly is typical.

How often should nappy bins be collected?

Usually weekly at minimum, more often for larger settings. Overflowing nappy bins are a hygiene and odour issue.
