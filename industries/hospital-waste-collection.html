Hospital & Healthcare Waste Collection

Multi-stream healthcare waste comparison for hospitals, clinics and treatment centres — clinical, sharps, offensive, confidential and residual — via licensed specialists.

Hospitals and large clinics run several regulated waste streams at once: infectious clinical waste, sharps, offensive/hygiene waste, confidential records, recycling and residual general waste. Colour-coded bags, consignment notes and registered carriers are non-negotiable. Stratos Environmental compares licensed healthcare-waste partners; we do not treat a hospital like a shop with a single 1100L bin.

Core healthcare streams

  • Clinical / infectious waste
  • Sharps
  • Offensive waste
  • Confidential waste for records
  • Recycling and general waste for non-clinical areas

Getting a stream in the wrong bag is both a safety and a cost problem: clinical disposal is far more expensive than offensive or general, so over-classifying wastes money while under-classifying is illegal.

Who this page is for

Hospitals, treatment centres, community clinics and larger dental/medical groups. Smaller single-surgery sites are usually better served by our dental page. We compare on price and on whether the carrier can actually service your site access and collection windows.

Frequently asked questions

Can hospital general waste go with clinical waste?

No. Mixing inflates cost and can breach regulations. Streams must be segregated at source into the correct colour-coded containers.

Do you work with NHS and private sites?

We compare licensed specialist partners for healthcare premises. Suitability depends on the site, access and the streams required — we'll be honest about what we can place.

What paperwork is required?

Healthcare waste typically needs consignment notes and Duty of Care documentation, retained as required. Licensed carriers we compare handle this as part of the service.
