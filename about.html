About Stratos Environmental Ltd

Environment Agency–registered upper tier waste broker and dealer. We put licensed carriers in competition for your business — honest advice, better prices, paperwork handled.

Get a Free Quote View credentials

Stratos Environmental Ltd helps UK businesses compare commercial waste collection. We are not a single bin company and we are not tied to one national carrier. We search a licensed partner network for the combination of price, frequency and waste streams that actually fits your premises, then we set the collection up once you are happy.

How we work

  1. You tell us your waste types, bin sizes and postcode — or upload a current invoice.
  2. We compare registered carriers and come back with options.
  3. If you proceed, bins are delivered, collections are scheduled and Duty of Care paperwork is handled.

The comparison is free. There is no obligation to switch. If your current deal is already strong, we will say so.

Where we are

Frequently asked questions

Are you a waste carrier?

We are registered with the Environment Agency as an upper tier waste broker and dealer (CBDU661179), not as a waste carrier operating our own fleet. Collections are carried out by registered waste carriers in our partner network. You still get full Duty of Care documentation. See compliance & credentials.

Do you cover the whole UK?

We arrange collections UK-wide through partners, with particularly strong coverage in London and the South East. We'll confirm your postcode when you enquire.

How do you get paid?

Like most brokers, we are remunerated by the provider when you take a service. That does not change the fact we compare multiple options and will tell you if we cannot beat your current price.
