Who we are

About Stratos Environmental

We are an independent commercial waste comparison broker based in Wandsworth. We put licensed providers in competition for your business — honest advice, better prices, paperwork handled.

Get a Free Quote Upload Your Invoice

Stratos Environmental helps UK businesses compare commercial waste collection. We are not a single bin company and we are not tied to one national carrier. We search a licensed partner network for the combination of price, frequency and waste streams that actually fits your premises, then we set the collection up once you are happy.

How we work

  1. You tell us your waste types, bin sizes and postcode — or upload a current invoice.
  2. We compare registered carriers and come back with options.
  3. If you proceed, bins are delivered, collections are scheduled and Duty of Care paperwork is handled.

The comparison is free. There is no obligation to switch. If your current deal is already strong, we will say so.

Where we are

We are based at Enterprise Way, London SW18 1GD, in Wandsworth, and we arrange collections across London and the wider UK. Coverage is particularly strong across south-west and central London — see our London guide.

What we will not do

  • We will not pretend unlicensed collectors are acceptable.
  • We will not hide contract notice periods or invent savings we cannot evidence.
  • We will not quote a bin size that will not physically fit your site.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a waste carrier?

We are a comparison broker. Collections are carried out by registered waste carriers in our partner network. You still get full Duty of Care documentation.

Do you cover the whole UK?

We arrange collections UK-wide through partners, with particularly strong coverage in London and the South East. We'll confirm your postcode when you enquire.

How do you get paid?

Like most brokers, we are remunerated by the provider when you take a service. That does not change the fact we compare multiple options and will tell you if we cannot beat your current price.
