Getting a business waste quote should be quick and painless. With Stratos Environmental Ltd it is: share a few details about your business and we’ll compare licensed providers to give you a competitive, no-obligation quote — usually with a specialist calling you back within minutes.

What’s included in your quote

  • Collection of the waste streams you need — general, recycling, food, glass and more.
  • The right bin sizes and collection frequency for your premises.
  • A fully licensed, registered carrier — Duty of Care compliant.
  • All the paperwork, including your waste transfer notes.

Why get your quote through us

We’re a broker, not a single supplier, so your quote reflects the best of the market rather than one company’s price list. We compare, we advise honestly, and if you already have a contract we’ll benchmark your invoice and try to beat it.

What we need from you

Just your business type and postcode, the waste types you produce, and a rough idea of volumes and how often you need collections. That’s enough for us to put together your options.

Fast, no-obligation setup

Most businesses can be set up the same day once they’ve chosen a quote — bins delivered, collections scheduled, compliance handled. There’s no obligation to proceed, and comparing is free. Prefer to see numbers first? Read our commercial waste prices guide or compare quotes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a business waste quote?

Fill in a short form with your business type, postcode, waste types and collection frequency, or upload your current invoice. A specialist will come back to you quickly with a competitive, no-obligation quote.

How much does a business waste quote cost?

Nothing. Quotes and comparison are completely free, with no obligation to proceed.

How quickly can collections start after I accept?

Often the same day. We arrange bin delivery and your first collection with the provider, with minimal disruption.

Do you cover my area?

We arrange collections UK-wide through our licensed partner network, with particularly strong coverage across London and the South East.
