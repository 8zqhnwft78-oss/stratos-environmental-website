First Mile is a well-known, London-founded waste and recycling company popular with smaller businesses for its sack-based, bin-free service. It’s a good fit for many city-centre SMEs with no room for wheelie bins — but it isn’t the only option, and depending on your volumes and location a different provider or a bin-based service may work out better value. Stratos Environmental Ltd compares the whole market so you can choose what genuinely suits you.

Why businesses look at First Mile alternatives

  • Volume economics — sack-based pricing is convenient for low volumes, but higher-volume sites can sometimes save with a bin or FEL-based service.
  • Price benchmarking — to check the per-sack cost still stacks up as your business grows.
  • Service mix — some businesses need containers, specialist streams or frequencies that suit a different provider.
  • Coverage — outside the busiest city centres, other carriers may offer better value.

Sacks or bins — the right fit for your space

The best solution depends heavily on how much waste you produce and how much storage you have. For a tiny central-London office, sacks may be ideal; for a busy restaurant or a site with a yard, bins or an FEL container often cost less per kilo. We’ll help you weigh it up. Frequently asked questions

What's an alternative to First Mile's sack service?

Depending on your volumes and storage, alternatives range from other sack-based recycling services to bin-based or FEL container collections, which can be cheaper per kilo for higher-volume sites. We compare both approaches.

Can you beat First Mile's prices?

Sometimes, depending on your volumes and location. Upload your First Mile invoice and we'll benchmark it against our licensed partner network, including bin-based options that may work out cheaper.

Are sacks or bins better for my business?

It depends on how much waste you produce and how much storage you have. Sacks suit low-volume, space-constrained city sites; bins or FEL containers usually cost less per kilo for busier premises. We'll help you decide.

Is Stratos affiliated with First Mile?

No. We're an independent comparison broker and compare many licensed carriers based on what suits your business best.
