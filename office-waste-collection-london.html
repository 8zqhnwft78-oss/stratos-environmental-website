What an office typically needs

  • Dry mixed recycling — paper, card, cans and plastics, usually the largest office stream.
  • Confidential waste — secure destruction of sensitive documents, with certificates for your records.
  • Food waste — for offices with kitchens, canteens or regular catering.
  • General waste — kept to a minimum once recycling is separated.

Compliance and sustainability

Under Simpler Recycling, offices must separate dry recyclables and food waste from general waste. Beyond compliance, many London businesses now want waste data for ESG and sustainability reporting — recycling rates, diversion from landfill, and carbon reduction. We can help you choose providers that supply the documentation you need, and of course handle your waste transfer notes and Duty of Care.

Frequently asked questions

What waste services does a London office need?

Most offices need dry mixed recycling, general waste, and — where there's a kitchen — food waste, plus secure confidential waste collection for sensitive documents. We arrange all of these together.

Do offices legally have to recycle?

Yes. Under Simpler Recycling, offices must separate dry recyclables and food waste from general waste. Workplaces with 10+ employees have been required to since March 2025.

Can you provide recycling data for ESG reporting?

Yes. We can match you with providers that supply recycling and diversion data useful for sustainability and ESG reporting, alongside your standard compliance paperwork.

Can collections scale as our office grows?

Yes. We tailor frequency and container types to your headcount and building, and can scale the service up as you expand.
