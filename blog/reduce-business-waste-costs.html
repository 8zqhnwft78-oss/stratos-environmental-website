9 Ways to Reduce Your Business Waste Costs

Waste is often an overlooked overhead. These nine practical steps can meaningfully cut your bill.

Get a Free Quote Upload Your Invoice

Commercial waste is one of those bills that quietly creeps up. The good news: it’s also one of the easiest to reduce once you know where to look. Here are nine proven ways to cut your costs.

1. Right-size your bins

Paying for a 1100L bin that’s half full every week is money wasted. Match your bin size to what you actually produce.

2. Review your collection frequency

Being collected more often than you need is a common source of overspend. See our guide on collection frequency to find the right balance.

3. Recycle more

Recycling collections are frequently cheaper than general waste, and separating materials reduces how much expensive residual waste you send to disposal. Dry mixed recycling makes this simple.

4. Separate food waste

For hospitality and offices with kitchens, a dedicated food waste stream cuts the weight of general waste — often the most costly stream.

5. Compress and flatten

Break down cardboard and compact loose waste so bins hold more before they need emptying.

6. Avoid contamination charges

Putting the wrong items in a recycling bin can trigger contamination fees. Clear signage and staff training pay for themselves.

7. Share bins if you’re a small site

Some multi-occupancy buildings can share collections, splitting the cost between tenants.

8. Watch out for auto-renewing contracts

Many waste contracts roll over with price rises. Know your end date and notice period — see how to switch providers.

9. Compare providers regularly

Prices vary widely between carriers for the same service. Comparing is the single biggest lever — and it’s exactly what we do. Upload your current invoice and we’ll try to beat it.
