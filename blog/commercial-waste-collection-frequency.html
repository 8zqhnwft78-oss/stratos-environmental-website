How Often Should Your Waste Be Collected?

Collection frequency is one of the biggest factors in your waste bill. Here’s how to get it right.

Get a Free Quote Upload Your Invoice

Choosing how often your waste is collected is a balancing act: too infrequent and bins overflow; too frequent and you’re paying for half-empty collections. Here’s how to find the sweet spot.

What affects the right frequency?

  • How much waste you produce — volume per day or week
  • Waste type — food and clinical waste often need more frequent collection for hygiene
  • Storage space — how many bins you can keep on site
  • Seasonality — hospitality often needs more in summer or over holidays

Typical frequencies by business type

  • Small office or shop: weekly or fortnightly general waste, weekly recycling
  • Restaurant or pub: multiple general and food waste collections per week, plus glass
  • Cafe: two to three times weekly, with food waste
  • Warehouse: depends on throughput — often larger bins collected weekly

Signs you’re over-serviced (paying too much)

  • Bins are regularly less than two-thirds full at collection
  • You’re collected multiple times a week but rarely fill up

Signs you’re under-serviced

  • Overflowing bins or side waste (which can incur extra charges)
  • Odour or pest issues from food waste sitting too long

How to optimise

Right-sizing your bins and frequency together usually saves more than either alone. If you’re not sure, send us your current invoice — we’ll review your setup and compare providers to find a better-value schedule.
