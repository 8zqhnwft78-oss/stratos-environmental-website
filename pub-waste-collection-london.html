Pubs and bars are glass factories. A busy London pub can get through hundreds of bottles a night, alongside food waste from the kitchen and general waste from the floor — usually generated late and needing frequent, reliable collection. Stratos Environmental compares licensed providers to keep your cellar and yard clear, your premises compliant, and your costs under control.

Glass first — but not only

  • Glass recycling — the defining stream for pubs and bars, collected separately for safety and recycling, often several times a week.
  • Food waste — essential for gastropubs and any venue serving food.
  • General waste — floor waste, packaging and non-recyclables.
  • Dry mixed recycling — cardboard, cans and plastics from deliveries and service.

Timed around late-night trade

Pubs produce most of their waste at the end of the night, and storage — often a cellar or small rear yard — is limited. That makes frequency and collection timing critical, especially in central boroughs with timed-collection rules and busy pavements. We arrange schedules and container types that fit how and when your venue actually operates, and can scale up for events and busy weekends.

Compliance for pubs and bars

Like all businesses, pubs must use a registered carrier, keep waste transfer notes, and separate recycling and food waste under Simpler Recycling. We take care of the paperwork so you can focus on running the bar.

Control the cost of all that glass

High glass volumes and frequent collections make pub waste costly if it’s not set up well. Comparing providers is the quickest way to cut the bill — upload your invoice and we’ll try to beat it. We cover pubs across London, from Clapham to Putney and the West End.

Frequently asked questions

How often is glass collected from London pubs?

Busy pubs and bars often need glass collected several times a week, sometimes daily in central London, because of the sheer volume of bottles. We match frequency to your trade.

Can collections be timed around closing?

Yes. We arrange collection windows and container types suited to late-night venues and restricted pavements, so waste isn't left out at the wrong times.

Do you collect food waste from gastropubs?

Yes. Any pub serving food should have a separate food waste collection — it's a legal requirement and keeps general waste costs down.

Can you reduce our pub's waste costs?

Usually yes. High glass volumes make pub waste expensive if it's not set up efficiently; comparing providers and right-sizing collections typically saves money.
